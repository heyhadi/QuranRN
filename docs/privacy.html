Privacy policy

Last updated 12 October 2026.

Wird is a personal project by Munawirul Hadi. It has no account, no ads and no analytics. This page says plainly what it stores, what it asks other services for, and what it never does.

What stays on your phone

  • Your bookmarks, notes, collections, reading position, Khatam and Hifz progress, adhkar and tasbih counts, and settings.
  • Surahs, mushaf pages, translations and other texts you have opened, so they open again without a connection, and audio you download or that was loaded ahead (a cache of at most 150 MB you can clear in Settings).
  • Your last known location, if you allow it, so prayer times and the Qibla compass work offline.
  • Reminders are scheduled on the phone itself. Nothing about them goes to a server.

None of this is sent to the developer. Removing the app removes it.

Services the app contacts

To show you the Quran, Wird asks these public services for content. They see your phone's network address, as any website would, and the request that goes with it:

  • equran.id: Surah text, Indonesian translation, Kemenag tafsir, doa and some recitation audio.
  • quran.com (api.quran.com and verses.quran.com): tajweed text, English translations, word-by-word, other tafsirs, mushaf pages and per-word audio. When you search for an ayah by meaning, the words you type are sent to it.
  • everyayah.com: recitation audio.
  • aladhan.com: prayer times and the 99 names. To work out prayer times it is sent your coordinates, rounded to four decimals (about 10 metres), and the calculation method you chose. It is not sent your name or any identifier from the app.

Each of these services has its own policy. Wird does not send them anything else, and it does not use advertising or tracking services of any kind.

Permissions

  • Location, only when you open Prayer and agree: for prayer times and the Qibla bearing. You can refuse it and still use the rest of the app.
  • Notifications, only when you switch on a reminder: for prayer, suhoor, iftar, the daily ayah and Khatam reminders.

Feedback

The feedback options in Settings open your email app or the GitHub website. The message they start with contains the app version, your phone's system version and the app language, and nothing you have read, saved or written. You can edit or delete that before sending.

Children

Wird collects no personal information from anyone, children included.

Changes and contact

If this changes, this page changes and the date at the top is updated. Questions: wirdquran.app@gmail.com, or open an issue.
